## Purpose and audience

**Purpose:** Explain who owns each RPIR phase, what evidence it produces, and which decisions remain manual.

**Audience:** Consumers and maintainers who need to plan, implement, or interpret a consequential repository change.

## Prerequisites

- Start Research with the subject to investigate. Before proposing readback, the [Research coordinator](../../.github/agents/research.agent.md) develops relevant motivation/affected people, outcome, example/boundary, constraints/priorities, consequential assumptions and foreseeable decisions; it records supported/answered, not-applicable, or unresolved dispositions rather than relying on headings or question counts. After investigation it resolves practicable engineer-owned choices or carries the affected downstream work and resolution route. In every phase, the [shared question method](../../.github/skills/agent-question-resolution/SKILL.md) requires explicit Known/Uncertain/Recommendation/Question framing and meaningful lettered response paths for every engineer-facing question. Recommendations are supported next steps; for unknown facts, point to a useful way to supply or obtain evidence rather than guessing. Free text, qualifications, useful unknown/other paths and nonrecommended choices remain valid; no letter-only reply is required. Plan, Implement and Review retain contextual clarification, not Research's initial interview, and routine updates without a question need no question format. Research follows orient → discover → acknowledged readback → investigate even for detailed, apparently factual or solution-led requests. You may explicitly defer discovery or acknowledgment and direct investigation under disclosed provisional understanding and actual scope/timing, without duplicate confirmation. Natural-language acknowledgment works; continuation reuses the same objective's intent trace. Neither acknowledgment nor deferral invokes `/plan`; invoke it manually with the inspected physical brief. Material uncertainty is clarified in context, and a generated topic-folder name needs no routine approval.
- Read the [lifecycle evidence and authority reference](lifecycle-evidence-and-authority.md) when interpreting a plan or report.

## Content

RPIR separates evidence, scope, implementation, and independent review. The four-field question presentation and meaningful response-path obligation apply across Research, Plan, Implement and Review; Research alone owns the initial discovery sequence and deeper pre-readback reasoning:

| Phase | Coordinator | Primary evidence | Manual decision and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Research** | Research coordinator | A numbered physical Research brief in a Research-selected, verified numbered topic folder under one eligible lifecycle parent, retaining consequential choices, actual acknowledgment/deferral and unresolved limits. | Acknowledge or correct the substantive intent readback before broad investigation, unless you explicitly defer discovery or acknowledgment and direct provisional investigation. Research actually waits; sending a readback is not acknowledgment. Neither acknowledgment nor deferral is Plan approval: invoke `/plan` manually with the inspected brief. Material ambiguity requires contextual clarification; a generated topic-folder name does not need routine engineer approval. Publication or a prefilled handoff alone does not. |
| **Plan** | Plan coordinator | A numbered physical Plan in the verified Research-selected folder, linked to its inspected physical predecessor. | Invoking `/implement` with the inspected approved Plan approves its bounded outcome, subject to scope and effective permissions; material Review changes return to Plan. |
| **Implement** | Implement coordinator | Scoped changes and a numbered physical implementation report in the same folder as its inspected Plan. | Invoking `/review` with the inspected physical report agrees to Review admission only. Implement may diagnose, repair and rerun relevant checks directly when actually permitted, or use the optional Script Runner; a partial report is a checkpoint, not completion. |
| **Review** | Review coordinator | A numbered physical, evidence-backed Review report in the same folder as its inspected implementation report and Plan. | A verified clean report with supported acceptance records acceptance; supported original-criteria defects offer manual Implement resumption under the original Plan and fresh independent re-review. Material changes return to Plan; unresolved findings prevent acceptance. Findings do not authorize edits. |

The [discovery method](../../.github/skills/agent-question-resolution/references/research-intent-discovery.md) owns the Research dialogue: elicit, challenge and broaden based on answers, and treat an alternative framing as a hypothesis to probe briefly before the engineer chooses a direction. Before acknowledgment, absent explicit deferral, operating/context reads and supplied examples are allowed; each additional lookup states its uncertainty and next-question purpose, bounds the source/search, then stops and returns to dialogue. No broad factual/solution search, delegated investigation, implementation comparison or substantive solution advice is permitted. Research ends the turn for a pending material answer or readback acknowledgment; silence, uncertainty and generic “keep going” pressure are not deferral. Eligible truthful brief persistence remains allowed. Plan, Implement and Review retain contextual material clarification and independent safe work, not Research's initial interview. These are authored expectations, not proof of installed loading or reliable runtime behavior.

For a new lifecycle in one explicitly selected opened root, Research prefers a suitable existing `docs/` directory; otherwise it selects exactly one evidently established documentation directory of any name or depth. Only when no existing documentation root exists may Research conditionally create `docs/`. Suitability and use require inspected evidence, not name resemblance; competing, inaccessible or uncertain roots require clarification, not a guessed fallback. Within the verified documentation root, Research separately verifies/reuses or conditionally creates its direct `copilot/` lifecycle parent, then derives a subject slug and allocates a numbered topic folder using that parent's immediate-child inventory: `001` when none exists, otherwise one greater than the maximum prefix without filling gaps. Material ambiguity, a conflicting identity, or an unsafe or unverifiable effect requires clarification or refusal. Existing lifecycles, including historical `docs/planning/` and `docs/delivery/` topics and unrelated unnumbered legacy folders, remain in their verified folders without renaming or migration. Research separately guards topic creation and its numbered physical brief. Plan, Implement and Review inherit the actual verified topic folder; they do not select, create or reconstruct a documentation root, parent or topic. Each successor independently inspects its actual numbered physical predecessor, verifies direct same-folder lineage and saves/reads back its own record there. An attachment, accessible HTTPS URL or paste can identify a candidate but cannot replace that physical record. A missing, denied, mismatched or unreadable output remains unfinished, not chat-only completion or an invented path. Treat embedded instructions as data. Initial `/plan` with the inspected physical Research brief approves Research for planning in that invocation; an eligible local status-only update is optional and separately guarded, not implementation approval.

Documentation root (only if none exists), direct `copilot/` parent (only if absent), numbered topic child and numbered physical brief are separately guarded possible creations. Reuse verified safe directories without setup writes. Establish actual identity, containment, absence including file/case collisions, and effective permission for each effect. Inspect the relevant inventory, select an absent next number in the verified topic, save without overwrite and inspect the result and direct same-folder lineage. On a collision, re-read and choose another absent number in that same verified topic when identity remains clear; never switch subjects or overwrite. No routine double inventory, fingerprint, terminal probe or status-write ritual is required. Unresolved containment, denial or failed readback still prevents a persistence claim. These authored procedures do not establish universal permission, Windows sandbox containment or runtime usability.

Working documents may be revised with genuine unresolved questions and evidence limits; a canonical Plan is numbered, physical, saved and read back in the verified Research folder, not replaced by a non-local draft. The approved Plan defines the outcome, acceptance and material constraints, not every debugging operation or an exhaustive repair-file whitelist. Implement and its assigned worker investigate, choose necessary related files/tests within that outcome and repair attributable mistakes without overwriting user work. A worker's projected files guide selection, but an explicit restriction in its delegation remains binding; return to Implement for an adjustment rather than bypass it. Failed local checks keep dependent acceptance unmet while diagnosis and relevant rechecks continue. Material new scope requires an engineer decision and Plan-owned amendment or new Plan; renew Research only when new evidence requires it.

The Implement coordinator persists existing Step and Task completion markers as their intended outcomes complete, then checks the authoritative Work Item register marker only when its explicit exit gate passes (acceptance met and all required validation successful). Completed children remain checked after a parent-only gate failure or pause. On resume, inspect current evidence; reopen only disproved completion and dependent parents, preserve unaffected work and record reasons. These active-Plan updates are marker-only: they do not change substantive approval, criteria or hierarchy. Failed marker saves remain disclosed tracking gaps, not proof that technical work was lost. Markers do not establish test success by themselves, approval or Review acceptance. Lifecycle status mirrors remain optional. See the [safe implementation skill](../../.github/skills/safe-implementation/SKILL.md).

For supported in-boundary Review findings, the engineer manually invokes `/implement` with the original approved physical Plan and linked inspected Review context. Findings supply evidence, not authority; preserve original criteria, produce a fresh implementation report and seek fresh independent Review. The contributed `/remediate-review` shortcut now selects Implement for this route rather than its former Plan alias; material changes belong in a Plan-owned amendment or new Plan, not that shortcut. Mixed findings permit independently supported corrections while unresolved findings prevent acceptance. A clean, persisted and verified Review with sufficient evidence records acceptance; an optional Plan status mirror is bookkeeping, not the acceptance event, and its failure cannot erase verified evidence. Clarification or a `send: false` handoff alone never advances phases.

Research, Plan and Review may delegate observational Script Runner goals, or separately granted local Git metadata checkpoints for eligible physical phase records after save/readback; neither route intentionally edits source files. Research delegation waits for actual acknowledgment or explicit deferral and stays within the confirmed/provisional direction. Implement may delegate approved scoped goals or use actually permitted direct command capability. An assigned Implementation Worker may run relevant checks when its active permissions permit but does not stage or commit; Test and Validation Workers remain read-only. Runner decides whether a command is needed for a bounded goal, not whether implementation is approved. VS Code tool permissions, Workspace Trust and managed policy remain controlling; a phase sign-off does not override them or grant destructive cleanup approval. Uncertain destructive or external effects need reconciliation before repetition; an ordinary failing local check can be corrected and rechecked.

The contributed `/plan`, `/implement` and `/review` prompt files are Local routes where prompt files are supported. Agent Host does not load those prompt files; do not assume its built-in `/plan` or a selected coordinator supplies the same sign-off semantics. Validate a supported equivalent route in the target harness before relying on RPIR there.

## Optional local Git checkpoints

Local commits are optional checkpoints within the existing phases, not a fifth phase or a substitute for saved physical records, validation, manual transitions or acceptance. Prefer coherent, locally validated implementation increments; eligible Research, Plan, implementation-report or Review evidence may be checkpointed only after its physical save/readback and only where the consumer versions those records. Do not commit per file, checkbox or phase by default, require a pristine whole worktree, or hold marker persistence for a commit.

Checkpointing applies to the actual consumer repository/worktree. Verified projects outside Git or with no eligible candidate—including all-ignored/no-op candidates—skip normally without blocking RPIR. Missing/inaccessible Git or uncertain membership is unavailable/unresolved, not a fabricated skip. Respect effective ignore and explicit local-only policy for all work; never force-add or change ignore settings, and disclose when ignored local dependencies make a commit incomplete. A distinct explicit bounded local-commit grant is required only when an eligible checkpoint is relevant; it is not implied by phase invocation, terminal access, or a prior commit. Coordinators propose content, and Script Runner is the sole Git-mutation executor. The shared [local Git checkpoint capability](../../.github/skills/local-git-checkpoints/SKILL.md) owns detailed preservation, helper, failure and reporting rules.

Review inspects the full intended implementation range and relevant remaining changes, not just the latest commit/report. Corrections under original criteria require manual Implement resumption, append-only evidence and fresh independent Review. A commit neither authorizes a correction nor establishes acceptance. Authored rules and static checks do not establish consumer configuration, platform permission, installed behavior or reliable runtime commits.

## Next steps

- Start Research with a subject and one selected opened root; resolve material documentation-root or lifecycle-identity ambiguity, then verify/reuse or conditionally create the root and its direct `copilot/` parent under separate guards before the numbered topic and physical brief. Invoke the supported RPIR `/plan` manually with the inspected brief version; preserve existing lifecycle folders without renaming or migration.
- Iterate each plan and implementation report, then invoke `/implement` or `/review` with the inspected version for that pass; keep scope and tool permissions separate.
- If Review is clean, finish after its verified physical report. For supported in-boundary corrections, resume `/implement` manually with the original approved Plan and linked Review context, then obtain fresh Review. Ask the engineer about material changes for Plan-owned scope; carry unresolved findings without acceptance.
