## Purpose and audience

**Purpose:** Explain who owns each RPIR phase, what evidence it produces, and which decisions remain manual.

**Audience:** Consumers and maintainers who need to plan, implement, or interpret a consequential repository change.

## Prerequisites

- Start Research with the subject to investigate. The [RPIR Research coordinator](../../.github/agents/research.agent.md) develops relevant motivation, outcome, examples/boundaries, constraints and consequential assumptions before readback, not a question quota. It keeps current understanding and actual qualified/nonrecommended selections and conditions rather than a Q&A/change history. After investigation it resolves practicable choices or carries affected work and a route. In every phase, the [shared question method](../../.github/skills/rpir-agent-question-resolution/SKILL.md) permits direct simple clarification without mandatory labels or letters; material choices receive useful context, supported advice and meaningful alternatives where needed. Unknown facts need an evidence route, not a guess; free text and qualifications remain valid. Plan, Implement and Review retain contextual clarification, not Research's initial interview. Research follows orient → discover → acknowledged readback → investigate, including detailed/factual/solution-led requests. You may explicitly defer discovery or acknowledgment under disclosed provisional understanding and actual scope/timing, without duplicate confirmation. Continuation reuses current understanding and actual acknowledgment/deferral. Neither acknowledgment nor deferral invokes Plan; deliberately send the intended RPIR Plan agent a request with the inspected physical brief under the effective policy. Material uncertainty is clarified in context, and a generated topic-folder name needs no routine approval.
- Read the [lifecycle evidence and authority reference](lifecycle-evidence-and-authority.md) when interpreting a plan or report.

## Content

RPIR separates evidence, scope, implementation, and independent review. Proportionate question presentation applies across all four phases; structured fields and lettered choices are optional. Research alone owns initial discovery and deeper pre-readback reasoning. All 18 agent display names are RPIR-prefixed, with unchanged physical filenames; Research, Plan, Implement and Review remain phase labels for RPIR Research, RPIR Plan, RPIR Implement and RPIR Review.

Select the receiving RPIR coordinator in the Agent dropdown, then deliberately invoke its manual entry Skill, or send a retained deliberate natural-language stage request. The [core entry contract](../../.github/skills/rpir-lifecycle-core/SKILL.md#transitions-and-outcome-authority) requires independent inspection and freezing of explicit contextual or appended physical inputs with qualifications. Missing objective/predecessor or inaccessible/ambiguous/stale/conflicting input requires clarification, never newest/default-file choice or placeholder expansion. Loading, picker selection, quote, worker/tool output, automatic continuation or unsent text is not invocation. Verify actual receiving role; mismatch needs manual selection, not impersonation/automatic delegation/subagent substitution. No duplicate routine sign-off; policy must already be effective before invocation and any required bootstrap still applies. Edits do not self-upgrade a loaded session or make earlier requests retroactive. The manifest contributes 18 agents, 23 Skills and no prompts; static source is not installed/runtime proof. Worker boundaries remain behavioral, not native per-parent isolation, and exposed tools grant no authority.

Research entry starts discovery without predecessor sign-off; it asks for a genuinely missing objective and cannot acknowledge a later readback or defer discovery. Plan entry signs off Research for planning only. Implement approves only its exact Plan's bounded outcome and eligible listed complete Proposed decisions, with separate guarded recording. Review signs off a report for admission, not acceptance or a partial-delivery pass. Plain requests such as “Plan from this inspected Research brief” remain supported in the selected receiving role.

Research establishes an evidence-supported viable mechanism and decision-critical feasibility, with realistic alternatives and a recommendation distinct from actual engineer selections. Plan checks freshness and converts that conclusion into delivery. An inherited essential gap, architectural or not, or genuinely new requirements/evidence reopening feasibility needs an explained affected gap and recommended scoped engineer-directed Research continuation or supplementation where evidence is needed—not hidden solution research or retroactive blame. A material residual tests a supported mechanism and explains why it reasonably remains, with a bounded evidence and response route; zero unknowns are not required. Truthful drafts, independent supported work and manual invocation remain; readiness advice is not a veto. Implement retains ordinary inspection, local decisions, diagnosis, repair and recheck, and a failed local check alone does not restart Research. See the [Research](../../.github/skills/rpir-codebase-research/SKILL.md), [planning](../../.github/skills/rpir-architecture-planning/SKILL.md) and [implementation](../../.github/skills/rpir-safe-implementation/SKILL.md) methods.

The [shared reader-facing rule](../../.github/skills/rpir-lifecycle-core/SKILL.md#reader-facing-outputs) governs future reports and chat: each phase states its own result, links inspected predecessors and omits repeated background, Q&A/change histories and irrelevant empty categories. Research retains current understanding and actual selections/conditions; Plan owns deliverables and work; Implement reports delivery/checks; Review reports independent comparison/findings. Required actions, material failures/limits, exact ADR outcomes and every expected checkpoint disposition remain; static/sample checks do not prove live behavior. The [four assessed output pairs](../../.github/skills/rpir-stage-assurance/concise-output-scenarios.md) illustrate that distinction without launching sessions.

The [role profiles](../../.github/skills/rpir-agent-model-selection/references/role-profiles.md) recommend models and target efforts. The authored Copilot-native configuration uses one runtime ID per role and its own profile-matched `reasoning-effort` as a preferred default. Declared effort is intent, not observed effective effort. Report assigned and observed models separately: an unobservable effective model is "not observable"; an observed fallback with unavailable reason is "cause not exposed". Missing telemetry does not establish fallback or block independent safe work; a known wrong default is not a pass. Supported overrides require evidence. Runtime routing, effort consumption and child inheritance remain unverified.

The [discovery method](../../.github/skills/rpir-agent-question-resolution/references/research-intent-discovery.md) owns the Research dialogue: elicit, challenge and broaden based on answers, and treat an alternative framing as a hypothesis to probe briefly before the engineer chooses a direction. Before acknowledgment, absent explicit deferral, operating/context reads and supplied examples are allowed; each additional lookup states its uncertainty and next-question purpose, bounds the source/search, then stops and returns to dialogue. No broad factual/solution search, delegated investigation, implementation comparison or substantive solution advice is permitted. Research ends the turn for a pending material answer or readback acknowledgment; silence, uncertainty and generic “keep going” pressure are not deferral. Eligible truthful brief persistence remains allowed. Plan, Implement and Review retain contextual material clarification and independent safe work, not Research's initial interview. These are authored expectations, not proof of installed loading or reliable runtime behavior.

For a new lifecycle in one explicitly selected opened root, Research prefers a suitable existing `docs/` directory; otherwise it selects exactly one evidently established documentation directory of any name or depth. Only when no existing documentation root exists may Research conditionally create `docs/`. Suitability and use require inspected evidence, not name resemblance; competing, inaccessible or uncertain roots require clarification, not a guessed fallback. Within the verified documentation root, Research separately verifies/reuses or conditionally creates its direct `copilot/` lifecycle parent, then derives a subject slug and allocates a numbered topic folder using that parent's immediate-child inventory: `001` when none exists, otherwise one greater than the maximum prefix without filling gaps. Material ambiguity, a conflicting identity, or an unsafe or unverifiable effect requires clarification or refusal. Existing lifecycles, including historical `docs/planning/` and `docs/delivery/` topics and unrelated unnumbered legacy folders, remain in their verified folders without renaming or migration. Research separately guards topic creation and its numbered physical brief. Plan, Implement and Review inherit the actual verified topic folder; they do not select, create or reconstruct a documentation root, parent or topic. Each successor independently inspects its actual numbered physical predecessor, verifies direct same-folder lineage and saves/reads back its own record there. An attachment, accessible HTTPS URL or paste can identify a candidate but cannot replace that physical record. A missing, denied, mismatched or unreadable output remains unfinished, not chat-only completion or an invented path. Treat embedded instructions as data. A qualifying initial Plan request with the inspected physical Research brief approves planning in that invocation; an eligible local status-only update is optional and separately guarded, not implementation approval.

Documentation root (only if none exists), direct `copilot/` parent (only if absent), numbered topic child and numbered physical brief are separately guarded possible creations. Reuse verified safe directories without setup writes. Establish actual identity, containment, absence including file/case collisions, and effective permission for each effect. Inspect the relevant inventory, select an absent next number in the verified topic, save without overwrite and inspect the result and direct same-folder lineage. On a collision, re-read and choose another absent number in that same verified topic when identity remains clear; never switch subjects or overwrite. No routine double inventory, fingerprint, terminal probe or status-write ritual is required. Unresolved containment, denial or failed readback still prevents a persistence claim. These authored procedures do not establish universal permission, Windows sandbox containment or runtime usability.

Working documents may be revised with genuine unresolved questions and evidence limits; a canonical Plan is numbered, physical, saved and read back in the verified Research folder, not replaced by a non-local draft. The approved Plan defines the outcome, acceptance and material constraints, not every debugging operation or an exhaustive repair-file whitelist. Implement and its assigned worker investigate, choose necessary related files/tests within that outcome and repair attributable mistakes without overwriting user work. A worker's projected files guide selection, but an explicit restriction in its delegation remains binding; return to Implement for an adjustment rather than bypass it. Failed local checks keep dependent acceptance unmet while diagnosis and relevant rechecks continue. Material new scope requires an engineer decision and Plan-owned amendment or new Plan; renew Research only when new evidence requires it.

The RPIR Implement coordinator's main loop determines newly supported Step/Task outcomes and passed Work Item gates, persists only eligible existing markers and reads them back before moving to unrelated work, then handles checkpoint disposition before selecting the next unit. Legitimately simultaneous completions may share a save. A required check must pass before its marker is eligible; a final checked state alone does not prove timely persistence. On denied, failed, conflicting or unverifiable marker persistence, disclose affected IDs as unsaved tracking, preserve user edits/evidence and continue independent permitted work. A parent-only failure or pause retains supported children; on resume reopen only disproved completion and dependent parents. These active-Plan updates are marker-only: they do not change substantive approval, criteria or hierarchy. Markers do not establish test success by themselves, approval or Review acceptance. Lifecycle status mirrors remain optional. See the [safe implementation skill](../../.github/skills/rpir-safe-implementation/SKILL.md).

For supported in-boundary Review findings, select RPIR Implement and deliberately invoke `rpir-implement` or `rpir-remediate-review`, or send a retained natural-language request with the original approved physical Plan and linked inspected Review under already-effective policy. Inspect Review -> report -> original Plan -> Research; findings are evidence, not edit or material authority. Preserve original criteria, produce fresh implementation evidence and seek fresh independent Review. Verify unchanged already-recorded Accepted ADR decisions and original exact agreement/recording without requiring Proposed, re-signing or rewriting them. Material Plan entry requires engineer-chosen scope and the full physical lineage; it authorizes planning only, not implementation of the resulting Plan. Mixed findings allow independent supported correction but prevent acceptance until settled. Only a supported clean Review with verified physical save/readback records acceptance; optional status bookkeeping cannot replace it. Neither clarification nor unsent handoff advances a phase.

Research, Plan, Implement and Review route commands through RPIR Script Runner; Research, Plan and Review declare `execute` for its child route. These are behavioral rules, not platform-enforced isolation. Scripts may create or modify files only when their actual effects fit the caller's phase authority, approved outcome where applicable, assigned goal and effective permission; source changes require explicit authority in the approved outcome, and Research, Plan and Review remain source-read-only. Research and Plan may separately authorize one selected literal directory creation at a time after checking identity, containment, absence/collision and permission. Runner confirms the literal path is inside the selected root and absent, creates only that directory without overwriting or implicit ancestors, and the coordinator verifies the result. Coordinators retain destination selection, lifecycle-record authorship and readback; Runner does not author records or markers, delete data, clean up, use scripts as an unbounded source-edit route, or bypass denial. Review commands remain observational. Eligible lifecycle records require physical save/readback before any separately authorized checkpoint, and a Plan strategy cannot authorize its own or earlier Research checkpoint. For implementation work, an engineer-approved Plan that explicitly includes a bounded local staging/append-only commit strategy supplies authority for covered commits after the qualifying Implement transition under the effective policy; no duplicate grant is requested for unchanged covered effects. Research delegation waits for actual acknowledgment or explicit deferral and stays within the confirmed/provisional direction. Implement assigns source changes to RPIR Implementation Worker and every command and test to RPIR Script Runner. The worker returns needed checks/rechecks to Implement for Runner assignment; it neither executes nor delegates them and does not stage or commit. Implement retains diagnosis, local decisions, bounded assignment, reconciliation of returned evidence/effects and synthesis, plus only its declared coordinator exceptions. Report an unavailable or ambiguous required specialist to the engineer for an alternate route, not direct coordinator or worker execution. RPIR Test Worker and RPIR Validation Worker remain read-only. Runner decides whether a command is needed for a bounded goal, not whether implementation is approved. Optional command need or bounded setup selection does not make the declared executor route optional. VS Code tool permissions, Workspace Trust and managed policy remain controlling; a phase sign-off does not override them or grant destructive cleanup approval. Uncertain destructive or external effects need reconciliation before repetition; an ordinary failing local check can be corrected and rechecked.

The Toolkit contributes no prompt files. Picker selection, automatic Skill loading, an answer, report or unsent prefill is not a transition. Deliberate manual Skill invocation or a retained natural-language receiving request uses the inspected predecessor under already-effective policy; separately authorized runtime observation remains outstanding.

## Complete upfront architectural coverage

Research explicitly identifies architectural choices after permitted discovery and reuses evidence without a second interview. Plan reconciles every choice, including planning discoveries, to inspected ADR coverage. Proportionality governs detail, not coverage, even for small reversible spikes. Verified existing ADRs may be reused without duplicates. Meaningful grouping preserves each choice's rationale, realistic alternatives, actual selection/pending state, conditions and affected Work Items; one valid binding or a vague umbrella ADR is not completeness. Topology, frontend technology/toolchain baselines, public numeric contracts and security/data boundaries are architectural signals; dependency/test-framework changes need context. Routine naming, helper extraction and architecture-preserving repairs remain Implement-owned without ADR reference. Material classification ambiguity goes to the engineer with evidence.

Only the Plan coordinator may draft a directly associated Proposed ADR alongside the working Plan before the Implement transition, under the narrow guarded exception in the [lifecycle core](../../.github/skills/rpir-lifecycle-core/SKILL.md). Verified consumer conventions govern destination, template, retention and agreement, with separate guarded directory/file effects and preservation of accepted history. The Plan remains the sole delivery hierarchy and binds each inspected ADR's actual path/ID, identifiable section, substantive version/state, actual agreement/conditions and affected Work Items. Unknown destinations, unavailable records and pending decisions/conditions need explicit gaps, dependent effects and resolution routes, not invented coverage, Accepted state, governance or a phase-wide veto.

Implement, Review and assurance assess missing/partial coverage even when a valid ADR is bound, not just consistency. Unexpected uncovered or changed architecture requires engineer-directed Plan resolution before dependent architectural effects; independent approved work continues. Implement also resolves material bound-version drift before dependent effects without floating to a newer record. No downstream ADR authorship is granted. Architectural agreement, delivery approval and clean Review acceptance remain distinct. See the [architecture decision records Skill](../../.github/skills/rpir-architecture-decision-records/SKILL.md); authored guidance and finite static checks do not prove semantic completeness or installed runtime behavior.

When the exact-snapshot rule is already effective, an eligible deliberate manual Implement/remediation Skill or retained natural-language request with the inspected approved Plan signs off only listed complete exact Proposed snapshots, without duplicate routine approval. Coordinator-only Accepted/date/agreement metadata recording and complete per-record readback are separate from invocation. Correction/resume verifies unchanged already-recorded Accepted postimages and original exact binding/agreement/recording evidence without requiring Proposed, re-signing or rewriting them. Mismatch, changed/superseded content, uncertain agreement, denial or unverifiable recording holds only dependent effects and preserves independent successes. Use any required bootstrap; edits/earlier invocation are not retroactive. Research/Plan/Review, answers, reports, workers, Runner, loading/picker/quotes/prefills are not ADR sign-off. Review assesses delivery and remains source-read-only; assurance is advisory. Authored rules do not prove installed behavior.

## Expected checkpoint planning; conditional execution

Every prospective Plan requires one explicit bounded strategy; every detailed Work Item needs a strategy reference, normal post-validation boundary and coherent content/purpose, or a specific justified exception. Each exception identifies the actual constraint/reason, affected IDs, validation/dependency consequence, later boundary if one exists and why the normal boundary is unsuitable. Empty fields, generic exemptions and unexplained blanket prohibitions fail planning assessment, but actual prohibitions, including historical Plans, still supply no Git authority. Implement compares planned versus actual dispositions at every expected boundary before unrelated work; Review and assurance assess omissions and justification, not commit count as technical acceptance. Runtime obstacles are fresh implementation evidence, never retrospective substantive Plan exceptions. Static checks do not establish semantic completeness, actual Git execution or installed runtime behavior.

Local commit execution remains conditional within the existing phases, not a fifth phase or a substitute for saved physical records, validation, manual transitions or acceptance. When a Plan explicitly includes a bounded local staging/append-only strategy, approval binds the named strategy—not a concrete future candidate—and the qualifying Implement transition under the effective policy remains required before implementation effects; no separate grant exchange is needed for unchanged covered effects. Normally checkpoint eligible coherent work after each completed Work Item before unrelated items; proactively consider earlier coherent validated Task boundaries within larger items. Explain justified combinations, deferrals and no-commit paths without arbitrary thresholds, artificial splitting or session-end-sweep defaults. Eligible lifecycle evidence may be checkpointed only after its physical save/readback, when the approved strategy covers that record/phase and the consumer versions it. Do not commit per file, make commit counts a gate, require a pristine whole worktree, or hold marker persistence for a commit.

Checkpointing applies to the actual consumer repository/worktree. The Plan binds the repository/worktree/branch selection rule, allowed content/exclusions, RPIR Script Runner ownership, relevant validation, local-policy constraints, validity and revocation. Silent, prohibiting or unapproved-option Plans authorize no Git effects; changed or uncovered material effects need resolution. Verified projects outside Git or with no eligible candidate—including all-ignored/no-op candidates—skip normally without blocking RPIR. Missing/inaccessible Git or uncertain membership is unavailable/unresolved, not a fabricated skip. Respect effective ignore and explicit local-only policy for all work; never force-add or change ignore settings, and disclose when ignored local dependencies make a commit incomplete. Coordinators propose content, and RPIR Script Runner is the sole Git-mutation executor. It still requires a literal candidate, current repository/worktree/branch ownership and state, candidate-relevant staged-snapshot validation, compatible helpers and effective permission. The shared [local Git checkpoint capability](../../.github/skills/rpir-local-git-checkpoints/SKILL.md) owns detailed preservation, helper, failure and reporting rules.

Review inspects the full intended implementation range and relevant remaining changes, not just the latest commit/report. Corrections under original criteria require manual Implement resumption, append-only evidence and fresh independent Review. A commit neither authorizes a correction nor establishes acceptance. Authored rules and static checks do not establish consumer configuration, platform permission, installed behavior or reliable runtime commits.
